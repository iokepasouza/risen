Risen

Risen Startup Page · a private morning page for one person.

Risen is a personal app that runs on its owner's own computer. Each morning it shows one calm page: the date, today's calendar, a short look at the inbox, and the day's world news.

What it does with Google

With the owner's permission, it reads two things, read-only:

  • Gmail message details — how many messages are unread, and the sender and subject of the important ones. It never reads a message body.
  • Google Calendar — today's events, in time order.

It cannot send, delete, label or change any mail, and it cannot change the calendar.

Who it is for

Only its owner. It is not offered to the public, there is no sign-up, and it shows no ads.
