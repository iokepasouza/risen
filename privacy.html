Privacy note

Risen Startup Page · last updated 18 November 2026

Risen (its morning page is called Risen Startup Page) is a private app used by one person, its owner, on their own computer. This note says what Google data it reads, what it does with it, and how to stop it.

What it reads

  • Gmail message details (Google permission gmail.metadata): the unread count, and the sender, subject, date and labels of messages. It does not read message bodies or attachments.
  • Google Calendar events (Google permission calendar.readonly): the events on today's date.

Both permissions are read-only. The app cannot send, delete, label or change mail, and cannot create, change or delete calendar events.

How it is used

The data is used for one thing: drawing the owner's morning page on the owner's own computer. It is kept only in the running app's memory, so the page does not ask Google too often, replaced at each refresh, and gone when the app closes. The app does not write it to disk or a database, does not send it to any other server, and does not use it to train any AI model.

What is stored

Only the Google sign-in itself, saved in a private folder on the owner's computer. It is never uploaded or shared.

Sharing

Google data is never sold, shared, transferred or shown to anyone other than the owner. There are no ads and no analytics.

Risen's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

How to stop it

Remove "Risen" at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Access ends at once. The app's own disconnect command also hands the sign-in back to Google and deletes the saved copy.

Contact

Questions about this note: open an issue on this page's GitHub repository.
